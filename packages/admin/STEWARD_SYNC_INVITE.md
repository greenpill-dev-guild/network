# Steward Sync Invite Copy

Use this as the contextual note before or after the Directus system invite. The
Directus invite email contains the password setup link; this copy explains why
stewards are receiving it and what to have ready.

## Subject

Greenpill steward sync: set up Directus and refresh your chapter profile

## Message

Hi stewards,

We are using Greenpill Network Steward Admin to refresh public chapter
information before publishing the next website snapshot.

Before the sync:

1. Open the Directus invite from Greenpill Network.
2. Accept the invite, set your password, and log in at `[ADMIN_URL]`.
3. Open Content and confirm you can see:
   - Chapters
   - Chapter Update Requests
   - Chapter Initiatives
4. Have these public-safe chapter details ready:
   - Current chapter summary or a better short summary
   - Primary public link
   - Public website, social, program, event, or contact links
   - Proof signals with public sources when available
   - Public image URL, alt text, and image credit if you want a new image
   - Local initiatives, programs, events, cleanups, education series, or Water Cup-style work

During the sync we will:

1. Walk through accepting the invite and finding your assigned chapter.
2. Review the current public chapter profile.
3. Open your pre-created Chapter Update Request draft.
4. Add proposed summary, link, proof signal, and media updates.
5. Add a Chapter Initiative if your chapter has local work to publish.
6. Move ready work to Pending Review so a trusted publisher can apply it.

Please keep private emails, private chats, wallet-only contact details, raw
notes, and unapproved claims out of Directus fields that publish to the website.

If your invite link is blank, expired, or your chapter does not appear after
login, reply in the sync thread and we will refresh the invite or assignment.
